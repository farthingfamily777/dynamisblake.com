My 2007 Senior Yearbook: The Pages I Designed

A treasure of the Farthing Archive. A time capsule from 2007.

FactArtifact · 2007 Senior YearbookCover and much of the design by Blake Farthing

This book is a time capsule. It holds the Class of 2007 just as we were: flip phones, Tetris in class, Friday night lights, and a whole school year told like a movie. Every page opens with a movie quote. I am proud of it.

Here is how it happened. I joined the yearbook staff my senior year to talk to a girl. It worked: she became my girlfriend. My friend since 3rd grade was named editor, and he was so busy that he let me design much of the book.

I made the cover, the senior section, the dividing pages, and the English and History spreads, and much more.
