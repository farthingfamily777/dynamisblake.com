# Talking to Your Towns: Couriers and Governor Reports v0.1

Status: DESIGN ONLY. Do not change game code from this file.
Date: 2026-11-10, evening CT.
Lead: Agon, from Blake: "How do I talk to them without the politics?"
House rules: rivalries first; factions can splinter; never show the house-forbidden digit run; no eye imagery; kid-friendly; plain words.

Blake wants to stay in touch with a town he founded, or with a partner town, without opening a full government sim every time. This system is that simple path. The deeper offices, elections, and constitutions live in `government-guilds-legions-v0.1.md` and stay behind a door labeled "Hall" for when he wants them.

---

## The idea

You write a short **letter**. A **courier** carries it (cart, ship, or later a starship pouch). Each season, or when you ask, the person in charge sends a **governor's report** with a few plain facts and two or three buttons. That is the whole daily loop.

---

## Phase-first version (Phase 4)

Phase 4 already adds one trade route to a second town, and a governor ladder. That is enough to hook this system. Do not add it in Phase 1 or 2, when there is only Port Paradise and you can walk across town yourself.

### The Courier Desk

A small desk in the market or the counting house. Later, every founded camp gets a post as soon as it is a camp.

You pick:

1. **Who it is for** — the governor, the founder you sent, a backer, or the Aedile (markets).
2. **A ready letter** — you do not type a speech. You pick one of these:

| Letter | What it does |
|---|---|
| Send grain (or another good) | Ships a little stock from your chest or the market. Costs money and a travel wait. |
| Send money | Moves coins to their chest. |
| Ask for a report | They send a governor's report back when the courier returns. |
| Call them home | Asks the founder or an official to return. They may say yes or no. |
| Hold a festival | Spends a little money; mood goes up (kid-friendly). |
| Change one law | Only the four charter lines: who rules, taxes, vampires, trade. This is a simple yes/no request, not a debate sim. |

3. **Send.** A travel timer starts (a few days). You can go play something else. When the courier is far away, one dice roll decides if the letter arrives safely. Near you, you can see the courier leave the dock.

### The governor's report

One page, readable for ages 7, 9, 87, and 90:

- **Food:** Low / Enough / Plenty
- **Money:** Low / Enough / Plenty (exact coins one tap away; never print the forbidden digit run)
- **Mood:** Upset / Steady / Glad
- **One problem** in a short sentence, for example: "The dock crew is angry at the Counting House."
- **One piece of good news**, for example: "A fish surplus. Prices fell."
- **Two or three buttons:** Help (send a good or money), Leave it, or Come see (fast travel later; in Phase 4 this just sets a map marker).

Kid mode hides the law button and uses the word pictures above. No gore, no scary "failure" screens. A Low food line reads "The cupboard is thin. They would like a sack of grain."

### Memory

- One open letter and one last report per town.
- No inbox of hundreds of messages. Old letters collapse into the drama log (the Gazette).

## Later version (Phase 5)

- More letter types: send settlers, ask for a treaty, request a cohort, invite a guild hall.
- A rival can send you a letter too (a banking clan, a vampire clan, a splinter settler). You still answer with plain choices.
- If a faction splinters, you may get **two reports** from the same town, each claiming to be in charge. That is the drama. You pick whom to answer, and the other side remembers.
- Radio or starship packet as a faster courier on the Neon Coast, still the same letter choices.
- The Hall door stays there for people who want the full constitution and office list.

---

## How this still makes drama (without a politics sim)

Blake's standing rule is rivalries first. The simple letters are how drama arrives, not a way to skip it.

- If you always "Leave it," mood falls and a militia rumor can start.
- If you always send money, a backer may say the town is soft, or a thief crew may try the chest (Wild autonomy only).
- If you change the vampire law by letter, the other side writes back.
- A courier can be delayed by a rival route, which is a Gazette line, not a new minigame.

---

## What this is not

- Not a debate simulator.
- Not a relationship-stat maze.
- Not email with free typing (mods may add more canned letters later).
- Not a replacement for walking into the room when you are in that town.
