The Sports Universe

One newsroom. Two big game leagues. One running record.

O'Faydin Sports Network (OSN) is Blake's sports newsroom for this game world. It keeps one running record of scores, teams, and stories.

“The scores tell us who won. The stories tell us why we're still talking about it at lunch.”

Every score and stat here comes from Blake's game screens or his own words. If a number was not shown, we leave it blank. We never make one up.

How the desk works

  • Corrections are treated as newly recovered evidence.
  • Scores, records, rankings, awards, titles, and draft results are never invented.
  • Non-game pictures Blake sends are fictional OSN commercials, not game facts.

Broadcast Desk

OSN TV, OSN Radio, OSN Newspaper, and OSN Internet.

Publications Desk

The Fleur-de-Lis Chronicle (the archive), the O'Faydin Sportsbook (matchups and results), the O'Faydin Scroll, and the Black Flag Ledger (fact-checking).

The hub, O'Faydin Sports Network, keeps the official record and sits with both desks.

Two main leagues

  • Fact NFL (Madden franchise), same continuous save, profile blakers1776. The record covers the 2026 season recap and the 2027 season through Super Bowl LXII.
  • Fact College football (College Football 27 dynasty, also called the NCAA 26 / Aggie dynasty in the logs). Season in focus: 2032, with history back through 2027 to 2031.
  • Draft classes and NFL careers are meant to carry from the college universe into Madden when evidence exists.

The 2027 NFL season and the 2032 college season have the most complete logged results.

Coming soon

  • The 2032 college playoff and national title are unknown.
  • The 2028 NFL season has not been logged.

College flavor voices (sometimes called CSN) are used for color, not for stats. Rumors and columns from them are never treated as scores or records.
