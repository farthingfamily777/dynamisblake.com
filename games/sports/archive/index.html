The 2031 Super Bowl: Saints 12, Browns 7

The New Orleans Saints beat the Cleveland Browns 12-7 in the 2031 Super Bowl at AT&T Stadium. Both teams came in at 13-4. This is the Saints' and Blake Rivers' second title in three seasons, after they beat the Texans 14-13 in the 2029 season.

Blake's account of the game: Rivers, the league MVP, was held in check most of the night, and the Saints had only two field goals, 6 points. Cleveland took a 7-6 lead on a short Shedeur Sanders touchdown pass after an interception. On the last drive, with about 36 seconds left, Kenny Darby dropped a deep ball, and Rivers was sacked on 3rd and 8. Then, on the final snap with 0:01 left, Rivers hit Darby deep for the game-winning touchdown. Darby is wide receiver number 19 for the Saints. On that catch he was next to Browns number 11, Yankey. A Browns number 28 celebrated a touchdown in the end zone. Per Blake, Kenny Darby was the Super Bowl MVP. Josh McDaniels, the Saints head coach, raised the trophy.

The 2032 Super Bowl: Saints 14, Texans 7

The New Orleans Saints beat the Houston Texans 14-7 in the 2032 Super Bowl at Reliant Stadium. The final board confirms the score and the records: the Saints came in at 13-4, and the Texans came in at 14-3. This is the Saints' third Super Bowl title in four seasons, after they beat the Texans 14-13 in 2029 and the Browns 12-7 in 2031, and it is back-to-back after last year. Houston has now lost two Super Bowls to the Saints. Blake Rivers, number 17, was the Saints quarterback, and C.J. Stroud, number 7, was the Texans quarterback. Josh McDaniels, the Saints head coach, raised the trophy, with the commissioner beside him.

Blake's account of the game: The teams were tied 7-7 at the two-minute warning. Azeez Al-Shaair, number 0, a former Defensive Player of the Year, anchored Houston's defense, and the Texans held Jordan Bernard, the league rushing leader, in check inside. In the last two minutes, the Saints leaned on Cedric Ed, number 27, who had come over from the Titans. Ed ran for a 20-yard gain to the Houston 17, then picked up a first down. After a reverse and a throwaway by Rivers with 1:12 left, Rivers hit Ed down the sideline, past Houston's number 33, for the game-winning touchdown. Ed scored both Saints touchdowns. Stroud got the ball back with 1:03 left and no timeouts, and moved the Texans to the 40 with 31 seconds left. The Saints' rush forced a 4th-and-16 throw that fell incomplete.
