A depth filler wins Super Bowl LXII

The Patriots beat the Giants 21-0 in Super Bowl LXII, and the MVP was Aiden Fisher, a 67-overall depth filler in only his second year. Blake Rivers of the Saints won league MVP and Offensive Rookie of the Year. On the same screen, a news flash said the Dolphins had just traded for Rueben Bain Jr.

Offensive Player of the Year Nico Collins reached free agency as a 99-overall Superstar X-Factor. He wanted 4 years at $41.9 million a year, and Houston was not even on his list of interested teams. Blake later said the McNairs did not want to pay anybody anymore: Collins and Danielle Hunter went to the Bears, and Dalton Schultz went to the Seahawks.

The Texans beat the Eagles 7-3 in Super Bowl LXIII at Allegiant Stadium, Houston's second title in three years. They had won LXI, the Patriots won LXII, and now the trophy is back in Houston. C.J. Stroud threw for 64 yards, one touchdown, and 66.7 percent; Jalen Hurts had 137 yards, no touchdowns, and 78.9 percent, and the Eagles' last snap came with 26 seconds left.
