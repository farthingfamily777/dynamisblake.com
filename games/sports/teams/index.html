New Orleans Saints

  • Quarterback Blake Rivers, No. 17, was a rookie: 99 overall, 6'5", 225 pounds. On draft night he was shown with a Saints No. 1 jersey. He wears No. 17 in season.
  • Kamryn Gholston was New Orleans' first pick of the 2027 draft, Round 2, Pick 1 (No. 33 overall). Later in the season he is listed as a tight end. The exact draft-card position label was partly cut off.
  • Finished the regular season 15-2, NFC No. 1 seed, with a bye. Lost in the Divisional round, 10-16 in overtime, to Arizona.

Houston Texans

  • Won Super Bowl LXI in the 2026 season, 21-15 over Green Bay. Woody Marks was the Super Bowl most valuable player.
  • Finished 2027 at 15-2, AFC No. 1 seed, with a bye. Points for 566, points against 248. Home 8-0. The road record was not fully visible on the screen.
  • Lost in the Divisional round at home, 0-6, to Indianapolis.

Arizona Cardinals

  • Finished 2027 at 10-7, NFC No. 4 seed. Beat Dallas 42-37 and the Saints 16-10 in overtime, then lost the NFC Championship 13-28 to the Giants.
  • Justin Fields, No. 6, started those two playoff wins. Trey Lance is also on the roster. How Lance arrived is unknown.
