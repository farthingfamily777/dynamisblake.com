The Aggie Dynasty

The Empire vs the Challenger, season 2032.

In College Football 27, Texas A&M is the empire and Texas is the challenger. The 2032 season is the one with the most complete logged results. Real years are used in this dynasty.

Every score and stat here comes from Blake's game screens or his own words. If a number was not shown, we leave it blank. We never make one up.

The empire through 2031

  • Texas A&M has 6 national championships through 2031. Which seasons those six titles came in is unknown.
  • A&M won five straight Heisman Trophies, 2027 through 2031: Marcel Reed, Arrington Maiden, Nathan Matlock, Deshawn Burress, and Matlock again.
  • In the 2031 postseason, Clemson eliminated A&M and is the reigning national champion entering 2032. Round and score: unknown.

Stars of 2032

  • Nathan Matlock, A&M quarterback No. 6, is a two-time Heisman winner (2029, 2031). During the Lone Star Showdown he reached 4,005 season passing yards. After the SEC Championship he broke the national career passing-touchdowns record. The new total was not shown. His signature play is called The Matlock Truck.
  • Alex Hendrix, A&M running back No. 18, redshirt senior from San Saba, Texas. Mid-season snapshot: 124 carries, 1,185 yards, 9.6 yards per carry, 14 touchdowns.
  • Lee Iyiegbuniwe, A&M receiver No. 5, was Player of the Game in the Lone Star Showdown: 4 catches, 115 yards, 3 touchdowns.
  • Texas standouts in the record include quarterback Paul Perkins (No. 13), receiver Evan Craft, and Jimmie Stablein (No. 16), who opened the Arkansas game with a 96-yard kickoff-return touchdown.
