Seedy Almalexia District

A Morrowind mod I built at 16, in 2006

Built in Danevang, Texas. Still archived in France twenty years later.

The Concept

Seedy Almalexia District adds a very poor neighborhood to the city of Mournhold in The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind.

It started as a simple player-home mod. There was no fitting place in the game for a run-down apartment, so I built a whole district around it. I released it as a master file so other modders could add houses, shops, and quests.

  • Created: 2006, at age 16
  • Version 1.0 · Requires the Tribunal expansion
  • French translation and testing: Redondepremière, Wolfen · Textures: Sita

From Danevang to France

In 2026, the district is still translated and available to download on La Confrérie des Traducteurs, a French Morrowind community. Other modders even built on it. These are screenshots of the live French pages, taken November 1, 2026.
